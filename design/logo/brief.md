# Design brief: yoshihiko.tech mark

- Deliverable: logo (lettermark only). No wordmark is part of this deliverable.
- Brand: the single letter "Y" for yoshihiko.tech, the personal tech blog and portfolio of a Japanese software engineer (Yoshihiko). Uppercase Y.
- Logo type: Lettermark.
- Style: completely new; do not reuse the old mark (a thin square outline with a Y inside). Modern, precise, geometric. Explore both monoline (drawable as a real neon tube) and bold filled forms; the user will choose after seeing both.
- Context: the site is being redesigned in an original "neon netrunner" style: rainy neon night city, HUD frames with chamfered (cut) corners, glitch transitions, terminal-like UI. The mark must feel native to that world without imitating any existing franchise.
- Colors: neon pink #F0457F and cyan #5CCFD4 on near-black #0B0812. Must also work as a single flat color (monochrome, `currentColor`) on both dark and light backgrounds.
- Usage: site header (22–30 px), favicon (16 and 32 px), and a large neon sign in the hero (rendered with SVG strokes and glow). Legible at 16 px.
- Avoid: katakana (the user dislikes the name in katakana); anything resembling Cyberpunk 2077 / Edgerunners logos, UI or yellow-cyan branding; the old square-frame-plus-Y look; gradients and 3D in the mark itself (glow is added at render time).
- Assumptions: concept images are rendered on the dark site background because that is the primary context; light-background behaviour is checked in the SVG phase.
